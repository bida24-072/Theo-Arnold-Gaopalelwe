Who I Am

Analytical by training.
A builder by instinct.

I'm a third-year Business Intelligence & Data Analytics student at Botswana Accountancy College, currently in Year 3 Semester 1, with graduation expected in 2028.

Outside of coursework, I build my own projects — including a mobile finance app called PulaTrack and an AI Readiness analytics platform. Through these, I've picked up practical experience with Python, SQL, Power BI, and React.

I'm looking for an attachment where I can apply my mix of analysis and development skills to real business problems. I'm particularly interested in finance environments — investment analysis, risk, and financial roles.

But I don't limit myself to one lane. I've also built websites for Botswana businesses — boutiques, restaurants, and lodges. Every project teaches me something new about craft, users, and what actually matters.

Vision

Building evidence that Botswana can compete globally.

The digital tools that power businesses around the world — e-commerce, analytics dashboards, machine learning models — shouldn't be reserved for companies in Johannesburg, Nairobi, or London. They should be standard here, in Gaborone.

Every project I ship — a finance app, a website, an ML model — is a small push toward that reality. Each one proves that we don't need to wait for someone else to build this for us.
